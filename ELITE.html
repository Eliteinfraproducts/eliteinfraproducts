Our Legacy & Vision

From Aruna Timbers to
Elite Infra Products

Starting our journey in reputed timber units back in 1997, our foundational expertise inspired the launch of Aruna Timbers in 2012, specializing in sawn sizes, door frames, teak panels, and carved door shutters.

Building upon decades of trust, Elite Infra Products was established in 2022 as an exclusive, state-of-the-art manufacturing facility in Sultanpur, Hyderabad. Spanning advanced automated CNC machinery and precision seasoning technology, our plant produces over 3,000 custom-designed door sets each month.

We partner directly with leading builders and infrastructure firms across Telangana, supplying precision-engineered solutions that combine structural longevity with architectural splendor.
